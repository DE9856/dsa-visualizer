Privacy

LAST UPDATED 3 SEPTEMBER 2026

The DSA Visualizer has no accounts, no database, no analytics and no third parties. It doesn’t ask who you are, and it has nowhere to put an answer. Everything you build in it stays in your browser.

What is stored on your device

The site saves three settings in your browser’s local storage, so it can remember how you like it between visits:

  • dsa-viz:appearance — your theme, contrast and colour-palette choice.
  • dsa-viz:sound — whether sound is on, and its volume.
  • dsa-viz:code-tab — which language tab you last read source code in.

That is the complete list. These sit on your own device, are never transmitted anywhere, and cannot be read by anyone but your browser. The site sets no cookies at all.

What you build is never sent anywhere

Arrays, graphs, trees and every other structure you create exist only in the page you have open. When you share a setup, the whole thing is encoded into the part of the address after the # — and browsers, by design, never send that part of a URL to the server. So your data does not reach this site’s host and cannot appear in anyone’s server logs, even when you share the link.

Exports are the same: GIFs, videos and printable tables are rendered in your browser, using the fonts already loaded from this site, and the file is only ever handed to you. Nothing is uploaded.

What does leave your browser

Nothing goes to a third party. Your browser makes no request to any domain other than this one — no font CDN, no script host, no image host, nothing. The two typefaces are served from this site, so loading a page tells nobody but the server you asked.

That leaves the server itself. The site is hosted by Vercel, and like any web server Vercel receives the requests your browser makes and may log them — typically your IP address, user agent, the file requested and the time. That is ordinary infrastructure logging rather than something this site asks for, and it is the only record of your visit that exists anywhere. See Vercel’s privacy notice.

What the site does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or session recording.
  • No advertising, and no data sold, shared or handed to anyone.
  • No accounts, sign-ins, forms or newsletters — there is nothing to submit.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, location or files.
  • Copy buttons write to your clipboard; the site never reads it. On a phone, some gestures ask the device for a short vibration.

Removing what is stored

Clearing site data for this domain in your browser’s settings removes all three keys above and returns the site to its defaults. Nothing else of yours exists to delete, because nothing else was ever collected.

Changes

If the site starts doing something this notice doesn’t describe, the notice changes with it and the date at the top moves. There is no mailing list to notify, so the date is the thing to check.
